# Admin guide (for the site owner)

## Log in
Open your site address followed by `/admin.html`, for example `https://yoursite.com/admin.html`, and sign in with your username and password.

## Add or remove photos
1. Under **Add a photo**, choose the picture, give it a title, and type a category (for example Portrait). Use the same spelling every time, because the category creates the filter buttons on the site.
2. Press **Upload photo**. It appears on the site straight away.
3. To remove a photo, press **Delete** next to it in **Your photos**.

## Change the About text and services
1. Scroll to **Website text**.
2. Edit the About heading and text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
3. For each service you can change the title, description and price. The price is just text, so you can write "From K1,200" or "K500 per hour".
4. **Button shows photos from** picks which category the service's "View photos" button opens. Choose "No button" to hide it.
5. Use **Move up** and **Move down** to change the order, **Remove** to delete a service, and **Add a service** for a new one (up to 6).
6. Press **Save changes**. Refresh the public site to see it.

**Reset to defaults** throws away your edits and brings back the original text.

## Read enquiries
Messages sent through the contact form are listed under **Enquiries**. Delete the ones you have dealt with.

## Good to know
- Resize photos to about 2000 pixels wide before uploading so the site stays fast.
- Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
